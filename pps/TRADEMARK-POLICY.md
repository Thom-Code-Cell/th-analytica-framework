# PPS name and trademark-use policy

Status: project naming and branding policy  
Owner / maintainer contact: TH Analytica, thomas@th-analytica.com

## Important status note

This policy defines how the official PPS project wants its names and compatibility designations used. It does **not** state that a particular mark is already registered in any jurisdiction.

Do not use the registered-trademark symbol (®) for PPS, Personal Privacy Signal, PPS Compatible or related TH Analytica marks unless a corresponding registration actually exists and permits that use.

## Project identifiers

The project uses the following identifiers:

- Personal Privacy Signal
- PPS, when used to refer to Personal Privacy Signal
- PPS Compatible
- PPS compatibility badges or logos published by TH Analytica
- PPS by TH Analytica

## Descriptive references

Accurate descriptive references are encouraged. Examples:

- "implements Personal Privacy Signal (PPS) Draft v1.0"
- "built to parse the PPS Draft v1.0 payload"
- "based on the PPS protocol proposal"

Such wording must not imply certification, endorsement, partnership or official status that has not been granted.

Where practical, link to the canonical specification.

## Official compatibility designation

The wording **PPS Compatible** and any official compatibility badge are reserved for the official conformance programme.

An implementer must not use the official compatibility designation or badge unless:

1. the applicable conformance requirements have been satisfied; and
2. TH Analytica has explicitly authorised that use under the programme then in force.

## Forks and modified versions

Forks are welcome subject to applicable licence terms, but a materially modified protocol must not be presented as the official PPS specification.

A modified project should clearly distinguish itself, for example:

- "based on PPS Draft v1.0"; or
- "PPS-derived experimental extension".

It should not use branding or presentation that is likely to make users believe the fork is maintained or certified by TH Analytica.

## Vendor extensions

Vendor extensions should be namespaced and separately documented. A vendor may not redefine official PPS semantics while continuing to call the modified behavior the unqualified official PPS standard.

## Compatibility badges

Official badge artwork, if published, may only be used under the current badge terms. Copying a badge does not create compatibility status.

## No endorsement by reference

Mentioning Meta, XREAL, Even Realities, Vuzix or another manufacturer in PPS documentation or interoperability work does not imply that the manufacturer has adopted, endorsed or partnered with PPS unless there is public evidence of that relationship.

## Future registration

TH Analytica may seek trade-mark protection for one or more project identifiers. This document should be updated after any filing or registration so the public status remains accurate.
