# TH Analytica Framework: ai.txt

This document summarises the core guidelines for AI Visibility and AI Readiness within the TH Analytica Framework.

### AI Visibility Guidelines

- **Structured Data**: Ensure all important information is encoded using structured data standards (e.g. Schema.org, JSON-LD) so that AI can parse and understand your content.
- **Semantic Consistency**: Use clear, unambiguous terminology across your site. Align your wording with commonly used concepts in your domain to reduce semantic friction.
- **External References**: Provide citations and references to external authoritative sources. AI systems cross-reference claims; independent validation increases trust.
- **Balanced Optimization**: Avoid over-optimizing solely for AI systems. Maintain readability for humans and naturalness for search engines to prevent algorithmic downgrading.

### AI Readiness Guidelines

- **Process Clarity**: Document your key business processes and workflows in a way that can be consumed by AI agents. Clear steps and roles reduce friction.
- **Entity Linking**: Map your organisation’s key entities (people, products, services) to consistent identifiers so AI can reference them correctly.
- **Agent Interoperability**: Ensure that data and documentation are accessible via APIs or standard formats so that AI agents can interact with your system.
- **Security & Governance**: Implement appropriate access controls and governance mechanisms to protect sensitive data while enabling AI operation.

For detailed examples and definitions, consult the framework documents and definitions.md in this repository.
